# MineX SMP — Ad copy

Reddit headline limit is 300 characters, but in-feed the first ~70 are what
actually get read on mobile. Every headline below is written to work truncated.

**Destination for all variants:** `discord.gg/minex` (unique invite code per
variant — see `campaign-brief.md`).
**CTA button:** `Join` for Discord-destination ads. Avoid `Download`.

## Writing rules for this audience

Reddit users have strong immune responses to advertising. The copy that works
reads like a post, not a pitch.

- No exclamation marks. No "Join the BEST server!!"
- No superlatives you cannot evidence. "Best" and "#1" read as noise.
- Lowercase or sentence case beats Title Case — it reads as a human typing.
- Specific numbers beat adjectives every time. "230 players on right now" is
  worth more than "huge active community".
- Say the catch. A line like "it's free, there's a store, nothing in it affects
  combat" buys more trust than pretending the store does not exist.

## Angle A — the offer

Direct. Tests whether the free rank alone moves people.

| # | Headline |
|---|---|
| A1 | Free rank for every new player on MineX SMP — claim it on your first join |
| A2 | New on MineX? The rank's already yours, you just have to log in |
| A3 | Every new player gets a rank free. No vote links, no referral chain. |
| A4 | We're giving the starter rank to everyone who joins this week |

**Body (optional, A1/A3):**
> MineX SMP just pushed a new update, and every new player gets the starter rank
> free on first join. Java: fun.minex.gg — Discord link below if you want to see
> what's in the update first.

## Angle B — the fresh start

Targets lapsed players, which on Reddit is a large and underserved group.
Probably the best-matched angle to the platform's demographics.

| # | Headline |
|---|---|
| B1 | Haven't played Minecraft in a couple of years? Server's running a fresh update. |
| B2 | That itch to start a new world again — we just updated, might as well |
| B3 | Looking for an SMP where the map isn't already strip-mined to bedrock |
| B4 | New update, active map, and nobody's claimed the good land yet |

**Body (B1/B3):**
> MineX SMP — Java, fun.minex.gg. New update just landed, so the map's open and
> there's still land worth claiming. New players get the starter rank free.
> Discord's linked if you want to look before you install anything.

B3 is the strongest line in this document. It names a real, specific frustration
that anyone who has joined an old SMP recognises immediately, and it does it
without making a single claim about the server being good.

## Angle C — community / anti-pay-to-win

**Only run this if it is true.** Check the store before using these. If the
store sells anything that affects combat, economy or world access, cut this
angle — Reddit will find out and the comments will cost you more than the ads
earn.

| # | Headline |
|---|---|
| C1 | An SMP where the store doesn't sell you an advantage |
| C2 | No kits that win fights. No ranks that skip the grind. Just the server. |
| C3 | We'd rather you stayed six months than spent £40 in week one |

## Angle D — low-key / native

Deliberately plain. These often beat polished copy on Reddit because they do not
trip the ad-detector. Pair with creative B (raw screenshot, no overlay).

| # | Headline |
|---|---|
| D1 | Small SMP, Java, fun.minex.gg — we're trying to grow it properly |
| D2 | Running an SMP and looking for more people to actually play with |
| D3 | fun.minex.gg — that's it, that's the ad |

D3 will either be the best or worst performer. It is worth the slot to find out;
self-aware minimalism does unusually well on Reddit, and it costs one ad to test.

## Test plan

Round 1 — angle test. One creative (A), four headlines: **A1, B3, C1, D1**.
Equal budget, 5 days, no mid-flight edits.

Round 2 — take the winning angle, test its four headline variants against each
other, and test creative A vs creative B underneath it.

Round 3 — scale the winner, refresh creative every 2–3 weeks. Reddit audiences
for a niche like this are small enough that frequency climbs fast, and the same
image stops working sooner than you would expect.

## Comment moderation

Reddit ads have comment sections, and on a server ad the comments are part of the
product. An unanswered "is this pay to win?" sitting at the top of your ad for
three days will cost more than the clicks are worth.

- Reply from the server's own account, as a person, within a few hours.
- Answer the pay-to-win question directly and honestly, including the parts that
  are not flattering.
- Do not astroturf with alts. Reddit users detect it, and it is the single
  fastest way to turn an ad into a post about your server being dishonest.
- If someone posts a real complaint, answering it well in public is better
  advertising than the ad itself.
