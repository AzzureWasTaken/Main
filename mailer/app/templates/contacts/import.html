Import contacts

Upload a CSV exported from Excel, Google Sheets or another tool.

File format

The first row must be column headings. Only an email column is required.

  • first_name, last_name (or a single name column) are used for greetings.
  • Any other columns are saved too, and can be used in emails, e.g. a company column becomes {{ '{{ company }}' }}.
  • Duplicate emails are merged. Contacts who unsubscribed or bounced stay unsubscribed, even if they appear in a new file.
